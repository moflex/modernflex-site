Half the creatives we featured this year made their best work on a phone. Not as a gimmick, but because it was the device in their hand when the idea arrived.

That changes the craft. Framing gets tighter, edits get quicker, and the line between making a thing and posting it almost disappears. Some people love that. Others are slowing down on purpose, shooting film or bouncing tracks to tape just to get a pause back.

> The tools are no longer the barrier. Taste is.

The tools are no longer the barrier. Taste is. Which is good news for anyone who has it and no money for equipment.
