Bristol’s reputation for heavy, slow, sound-system music is older than most of the people now making it. For the producers we spoke to, it’s less a legacy to protect than furniture they grew up around.

They mix it with whatever else is in their headphones: amapiano drums, garage swing, the long silences of ambient records. Tracks are tested at small nights first and uploaded later, if at all.

> I make tunes for one room. If it works there, it works anywhere.

What links them is the room. Every one of them talked about the physical feeling of bass under a low ceiling, and about writing for a specific crowd on a specific night.
