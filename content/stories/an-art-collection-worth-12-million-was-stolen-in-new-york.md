A whole art collection, valued at around $12 million, has disappeared from a storage facility in Queens, New York.

The owner, 62-year-old retired collector William Pordy, hadn’t visited the unit between late October and the start of June. When he went back to pick up a $10,000 painting as a gift for a friend’s daughter, he found the locks cut and every one of his 22 works gone.

The collection included pieces by the French artist Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec and the American minimalist painter Frank Stella.
