2026 has been a big year to be in a cinema seat. By mid-summer, US ticket sales were running about 10% ahead of 2025, and Gen Z was back in theatres. Here’s what critics rated highest, what we’d actually watch, and what to put in next year’s calendar.

## Films of 2026

**The Odyssey** (Christopher Nolan). An R-rated, almost three-hour adaptation of Homer, with Matt Damon as Odysseus alongside Tom Holland, Anne Hathaway and Zendaya. It became the best-reviewed film of Nolan’s career on Rotten Tomatoes and opened to $264 million worldwide. If you see one film on the biggest screen you can find this year, make it this.

**Project Hail Mary** (Phil Lord and Christopher Miller). Ryan Gosling plays a science teacher sent to save a dying sun, with an alien sidekick who steals the film. Funny, warm, and now streaming on Prime Video.

**Disclosure Day** (Steven Spielberg). Spielberg back in alien territory, with a politically charged story that asks whether his faith in people still holds up today.

**The Christophers** (Steven Soderbergh). Ian McKellen and Michaela Coel in an art-forgery film. A good one to pair with our [piece on this year’s museum heists](/stories/the-heist-wave/).

**Rose of Nevada** (Mark Jenkin). Two crewmen on a Cornish fishing boat come home to find their village 30 years in the past, and everyone thinks they’re men who went missing. Shot on 16mm, it’s the most British thing on this list, and one of the strangest.

**Nirvanna the Band the Show the Movie** (Matt Johnson). Two slacker friends use time travel to try to land a gig at a Toronto venue. A cult TV show turned into one of the year’s funniest films.

**EPiC: Elvis Presley in Concert** (Baz Luhrmann). Luhrmann dug 59 hours of unseen 1970s concert footage out of the Warner Bros archive and restored it. The result plays like a front-row seat, not an archive project.

**Still to come:** *Dune: Part Three* lands on 18 December, closing Denis Villeneuve’s trilogy with an adaptation of *Dune Messiah*. It opens the same day as *Avengers: Doomsday*, which fans have already named “Dunesday”.

## Series of 2026

- **The Pitt** (HBO Max). Season 2 of the ER drama kept the standard high, with Noah Wyle at the centre.
- **Industry** (HBO). The finance drama keeps getting better, and season 4 raised the stakes again.
- **Hacks** (HBO Max). A farewell season built on Jean Smart and Hannah Einbinder’s relationship.
- **A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms** (HBO). The Westeros spin-off nobody expected to be this funny.
- **The Comeback** (HBO). Lisa Kudrow’s Valerie Cherish, first seen in 2005, returned for a final season, now starring in a series written by AI.
- **Beef** (Netflix). Season 2 of the feud anthology, this time about privilege, with a new cast.
- **East of Eden** (Netflix). A seven-episode Steinbeck adaptation, led by Florence Pugh.
- **Carrie** (Prime Video). Mike Flanagan’s Stephen King adaptation, all performances and heart.
- **Star City** (Apple TV). The *For All Mankind* spin-off, part Soviet space race, part spy thriller.

## Coming in 2027: films

- **Narnia: The Magician’s Nephew** (Greta Gerwig). In cinemas 12 February, then on Netflix from 2 April.
- **The Legend of Zelda** (Wes Ball). The live-action Hyrule, out 30 April.
- **Star Wars: Starfighter**. An original Lucasfilm story starring Ryan Gosling, 28 May.
- **Spider-Man: Beyond the Spider-Verse**. The end of Miles Morales’s trilogy, 18 June.
- **Man of Tomorrow**. David Corenswet’s Superman teams up with Nicholas Hoult’s Lex Luthor to face Brainiac, 9 July.
- **The Batman Part II**. The return to Gotham, also due in 2027.
- **The new Daniels film**. The *Everything Everywhere All at Once* duo, with a cast including Matt Damon, Sandra Oh, Charles Melton and PinkPantheress, 19 November.
- **Avengers: Secret Wars** and **The Lord of the Rings: The Hunt for Gollum**. Both on 17 December. Robert Downey Jr. plays Doctor Doom in one; Andy Serkis directs and returns as Gollum in the other.

## Coming in 2027: series

- **The Pitt**, season 3, January.
- **Industry**, the fifth and final season.
- **The White Lotus**, season 4, set in the south of France during the Cannes Film Festival.
- **The Last of Us**, season 3.
- **Heated Rivalry**, season 2, April.
- **Girls**, a five-episode reunion with the original cast. Filming starts in 2027.
- **The Studio**, season 2 of Seth Rogen’s Hollywood satire.

> If you watch one film and one series before the year ends: *The Odyssey* and *The Pitt*.
