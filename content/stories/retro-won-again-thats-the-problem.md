Look at what sold out this year and you’ll see the same story: a silhouette from decades ago, a new colourway, a queue around the block. Brands have learned that the past is the safest bet in the building.

There’s nothing wrong with a classic. But when every big release is a reissue, nobody is designing the shoe people will be nostalgic for in twenty years.

> Nostalgia is a great product and a terrible plan.

The most exciting footwear right now is coming from small labels and independent designers taking risks the giants won’t. Pay attention to them before the reissue machine does.
