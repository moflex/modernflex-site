Jean-Michel Basquiat’s *Untitled*, a 1982 painting of a skull-like face in oil stick, acrylic and spray paint, has sold for $110 million at Sotheby’s in New York.

The sale set a string of records at once: the most ever paid for a Basquiat, for a work by any American artist, and for a work by a Black artist. It’s also the first artwork made after 1980 to sell for more than $100 million. Pre-sale estimates had put it around $60 million, and the painting had rarely been seen since it last changed hands in 1984.

The buyer was Yusaku Maezawa, the 41-year-old Japanese fashion entrepreneur who had also set the previous Basquiat record a year earlier, paying $57.3 million.

Basquiat is also getting a retrospective at London’s Barbican, his first major exhibition in the UK.
