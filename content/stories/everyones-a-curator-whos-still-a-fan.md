Scroll any feed and you’ll find people arranging culture beautifully: grids of film stills, playlists for very specific weather, ranked lists of albums they’ve had for a week.

Curation used to be a job. Now it’s the default way of showing taste, and it rewards distance. The cool move is to present, never to gush.

> The best writing comes from someone who couldn’t keep quiet about a thing they loved.

We’d like a little more gushing. The best cultural writing has always come from someone who couldn’t keep quiet about a thing they loved. That’s the energy we want on these pages.
