Dami Osei has been photographing the same group of friends for six years: birthdays, raves, house moves, bad haircuts. The archive is the most honest record of young Bristol we’ve seen.

“People talk about access like it’s something you negotiate,” she says. “I’ve never negotiated. I’m just there, because I’d be there anyway.”

> I’m just there, because I’d be there anyway.

She shoots on a compact film camera and a phone, prints everything, and keeps the prints in shoeboxes sorted by year. She’s in no hurry to show it all. “It isn’t finished. They’re still my friends.”
