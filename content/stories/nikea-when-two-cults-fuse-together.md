What started as an internet trend mocking Balenciaga grew into an unmissable fashion statement.

When Balenciaga showed its $2,145 tote bag, which looked a lot like IKEA’s FRAKTA bag, the internet went crazy redesigning clothing and accessories with FRAKTA bags as the base material. The IKEA bag costs $0.99. The IKEA-inspired designs ranged from underwear made from FRAKTA bags to Yeezy Boost 350 V2s painted blue with “IKEA” written where “SPLY-350” usually sits.

What Nikea™ did was bold but ordinary enough for everyone to wear. By fusing two iconic brands it landed on a seamless new name. So far, Nikea™ sells two T-shirts in IKEA yellow and blue with the brand across the chest. As well as the name, they carry the date and place IKEA was founded, the word “BOXED” and the Nike swoosh. Even the tags look like a mix of an IKEA price tag and receipt, with the Swedish brand mentioned at the bottom.

You could find the Nikea™ SS17 collection on Instagram at @nikeawear.
