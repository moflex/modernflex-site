We spent four mornings on the harbour this autumn, starting before sunrise and stopping when the first tourists arrived.

The cranes are the only things that stay still. Everything else is moving: a rowing eight cutting through the mist, a bin lorry reversing for a full minute, two friends sharing chips on a bench at ten past six.

> The cranes are the only things that stay still.

These pictures aren’t the Bristol on postcards. They’re the hour when the city is still deciding what kind of day it’s going to have.
