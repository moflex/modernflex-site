Welcome to MODERNFLEX. Started as an Instagram page, we finally launched our website.

Here we focus on finding and writing about people with talent who want to be discovered. Although we also write about bigger names, such as established artists, musicians, entertainers and public figures, the main goal of this platform is to give people an opportunity to share their content with the MODERNFLEX community.

To be featured, contact us through our website or social media and tell us your story. We want original creators to get the attention they deserve.

> Be creative. Be inspired. Be you.
